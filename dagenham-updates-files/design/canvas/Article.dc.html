A13 westbound closed near Goresbrook after collision

Diversions are in place via Ripple Road. Several bus routes are affected and drivers are being asked to avoid the area while recovery work continues.
Published 08:42 Updated 09:15 Source: [SOURCE]

Featured image
Recovery vehicles on the westbound carriageway. [PHOTO CREDIT]

The westbound carriageway was closed shortly after 08:00 following a collision between two vehicles. Emergency services attended and the road remains shut between the two nearest junctions while the scene is cleared.

Traffic is being diverted via Ripple Road, which is already busy at this time of the morning. Drivers heading into town are advised to allow extra time or use an alternative route.

Please avoid the area if you can — the diversion is adding a good twenty minutes.
[ATTRIBUTION]

Bus routes through the area are on diversion and some stops are not being served. We will update this page when the road reopens.
