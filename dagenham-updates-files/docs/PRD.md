# Dagenham Updates — Product Requirements Document

**Version:** v1.0 · **Product:** Dagenham Updates
**Purpose:** A local news and updates platform covering Barking & Dagenham, with reach into surrounding areas of East London and Essex where relevant. The website acts as the main hub for local updates, community information, events, travel disruption, council notices and public-interest stories.

---

## 1. Product Overview

**Summary.** Dagenham Updates is an independent local information service designed to keep residents informed about what is happening in and around Barking & Dagenham. The website supports the brand's social media presence by acting as: a home base for all updates; an archive of posts and articles; a trust layer for the brand; a search-friendly destination for local residents; and a contact point for tips, submissions and enquiries.

**Vision.** To become the most useful and trusted independent local updates source for Dagenham and the wider Barking & Dagenham community.

**Mission.** To provide fast, clear, factual and community-focused updates that help residents stay informed and engaged.

**Primary goal.** Build a credible local media-style website that supports growth across Instagram, X/Twitter and search engines.

## 2. Product Goals

**Business goals.** Establish Dagenham Updates as a recognisable local brand; build audience trust and repeat traffic; increase social media followers through website cross-linking; create a searchable archive of local updates; enable future monetisation through ads, sponsorships and promoted listings.

**User goals.** Visitors should be able to quickly understand what the site is about; view the latest local updates; find information on local news, events, travel and community notices; contact the team or submit tips; follow the brand on social media; and trust that the content is local, useful and reasonably verified.

## 3. Target Audience

**Primary:** residents of Barking & Dagenham; commuters in and around the borough; local workers and business owners; families looking for community updates; people interested in local events and public notices.

**Secondary:** nearby East London and Essex residents; local journalists and creators; community organisers; small businesses; schools, clubs and event organisers; borough stakeholders and public service users.

## 4. Problem Statement

There is no single easy-to-use independent local updates hub for Dagenham that combines news, travel alerts, community notices, event discovery, public information and social media presence. Residents often need to check multiple sources. This website reduces friction by bringing the most useful updates into one place.

## 5. Product Principles

Fast — users should find the latest update immediately. Clear — no clutter, no confusion. Local — always focused on Barking & Dagenham and nearby relevant areas. Trustworthy — factual, sourced and careful. Mobile-first — most users will visit on phones. Search-friendly — structured for SEO from day one. Scalable — easy to expand as the brand grows.

## 6. Scope

**In scope for MVP:** homepage; latest updates feed; category pages; individual article pages; about page; contact page; submit-a-tip form; social media links; basic SEO structure; privacy policy; terms of use; cookie notice if needed; mobile-responsive design.

**Out of scope for MVP:** user accounts; comment system; membership/paywall; full newsletter automation; push notifications; live map or complex geolocation tools; multi-author CMS workflows; video publishing platform; full ad marketplace. These can come later.

## 7. Core Website Structure

**Sitemap:** Home · Latest Updates · News · Travel · Community · Events · Business · About · Contact / Submit a Tip · Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions.

**Optional future pages:** Weather Alerts · Schools · Police & Safety · Council Notices · Archive · Advertise With Us.

## 8. Functional Requirements

**8.1 Homepage.** Display the latest important update at the top; show recent posts in reverse chronological order; present clear navigation to key sections; include a short brand intro; highlight social media links; include a call-to-action for submissions/tips; display featured or urgent updates prominently. Sections: header/navigation, hero with brand statement, featured/latest story, category cards or quick links, recent updates feed, popular or trending updates, contact/tip submission CTA, footer.

**8.2 Latest updates feed.** List all recent posts in descending order; show title, date, category and preview text; allow filtering by category; be easy to scan on mobile.

**8.3 Category pages.** Group content by topic; help users find relevant updates quickly; support SEO for topic-specific search terms. Categories: News, Travel, Community, Events, Business.

**8.4 Article pages.** Headline; publish date/time; category; article body; featured image if available; source or attribution when relevant; social share options; related articles; CTA to follow on Instagram/X. Optional: "updated at" label, source links, disclaimer if information is developing.

**8.5 About page.** Explain who Dagenham Updates is, what the brand covers, why it exists, how information is sourced and how residents can contribute tips or notices. This page is important for trust.

**8.6 Contact / Submit a Tip.** Allow users to send general enquiries, submit a local story, share event information, report news tips and request corrections. Minimum fields: name, email, subject, message, optional attachment upload, category/type of submission. Optional: anonymous tip option, confirmation message after submission.

**8.7 Social integration.** Prominently link to Instagram and X/Twitter (Facebook if used later). Social embeds can come later; basic linking is enough for launch.

## 9. Content Strategy

**Content types:** local news; breaking updates; transport/travel alerts; community notices; event listings; business updates; council-related notices; safety/weather alerts; human-interest stories.

**Editorial tone:** factual, concise, local, useful, not sensational unless necessary, respectful of residents and institutions.

**Source policy:** prefer official council sources, transport sources, police/fire updates, event organisers, business owners and verified community submissions. If a story is not confirmed, label it accordingly.

## 10. User Experience Requirements

**Design principles:** clean layout; readable typography; strong mobile responsiveness; quick access to latest updates; simple navigation; minimal distraction; local news feel, not corporate blog feel.

**UX priorities, in order:** 1. find latest update fast · 2. understand what the site is · 3. access categories easily · 4. contact the team easily · 5. trust the information.

**Mobile.** Most users arrive from Instagram and mobile search, so navigation must be thumb-friendly, cards must stack neatly, text must be easy to read and buttons must be obvious.

## 11. Visual Design Requirements

**Brand look:** modern, local, trustworthy, simple, community-focused.

**Suggested style direction:** clean white or light background; strong accent colour for highlights; bold headlines; simple cards for article previews; minimal clutter; consistent spacing; logo and brand identity visible but not overpowering.

**Accessibility:** high contrast text; readable font sizes; alt text for images; keyboard navigability; good colour contrast for buttons and links.

## 12. SEO Requirements

**Technical:** fast page loading; mobile-friendly; clean URLs; meta titles and descriptions; heading hierarchy; sitemap.xml; robots.txt; canonical tags; structured data where appropriate.

**Content:** each article targets local search intent — Dagenham news; Barking & Dagenham updates; local events in Dagenham; road closures in Barking & Dagenham; community notices Dagenham.

**Internal linking:** between articles, categories, related posts, about/contact pages and social channels.

## 13. Trust & Safety

**Content safety:** no publishing rumours as fact; no defamatory claims; no unverified accusations; no doxxing; no harmful misinformation; corrections should be possible and visible.

**Editorial disclaimers:** state clearly that the site is independent; that updates are sourced and checked where possible; that users should verify urgent information with official sources; and that corrections can be requested.

**Privacy:** if users submit tips, avoid exposing personal data, store data securely, keep forms minimal and include a privacy policy.

## 14. CMS Requirements

Minimum features: create/edit/delete posts; assign categories; upload featured images; schedule posts; draft/publish workflow; update post status; manage homepage featured content. Posting must be fast — speed matters.

## 15. Admin Requirements

The admin dashboard should allow post management, category management, featured post control, a submission inbox or form responses, image uploads, SEO field editing, page editing, and user role management if needed later.

## 16. Analytics Requirements

Track page views; unique visitors; top-performing posts; traffic sources; click-through to social channels; submissions received; search performance; bounce rate; mobile vs desktop usage.

## 17. Growth Features (Phase 2)

Newsletter signup; email alerts; push notifications; sponsored business listings; event calendar; archive search; featured local businesses; live travel alerts; anonymous submission option; Instagram/X post embeds; map-based local coverage pages.

## 18. Success Metrics

**Launch:** website live with full core structure; 10–20 initial posts/pages; homepage and categories working; social links visible; tip/contact form working; smooth mobile usability.

**Ongoing:** repeat visitors; social follows from website traffic; submissions from residents; rising organic search traffic; increased engagement on updates; improved local recognition.

## 19. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Not enough content | Batch content in advance and maintain source lists |
| Losing trust through incorrect posts | Verify before publishing; add a correction process |
| Website feels empty | Launch with enough content and featured posts |
| Too broad coverage | Keep Barking & Dagenham as the core; widen only when relevant |
| Overly busy design | Prioritise clarity and simplicity |
| Slow posting workflow | Build easy article templates and CMS shortcuts |

## 20. Launch Checklist

Domain and hosting ready · logo and brand assets uploaded · homepage complete · about page live · contact/tip form working · privacy policy live · categories created · first batch of posts ready · social links connected · mobile view tested · SEO titles/descriptions set.

## 21. Recommended MVP Build Order

1. Homepage · 2. Category structure · 3. Latest updates page · 4. Article template · 5. About page · 6. Contact / submit tip page · 7. Privacy policy and terms · 8. Social links · 9. SEO setup · 10. Analytics tracking.

## 22. Product Statement

Dagenham Updates is an independent local news and updates platform serving Barking & Dagenham and surrounding communities. The website provides timely, clear and trustworthy information on local news, travel, events, community notices and other updates relevant to residents and businesses.

# Homepage Wireframe

**1. Header / navigation.** Left: logo / site name. Centre or right: nav links — Home, Latest Updates, News, Travel, Community, Events, Business, About, Contact. Far right: social icons or a "Follow us" button. Optional: search icon, "Submit a Tip" button. Keep it simple and sticky on scroll if possible.

**2. Hero.** Large headline, short subheading, one or two CTAs.
Headline: *Local updates for Barking & Dagenham and beyond.*
Subheading: *News, travel, community notices, events, and useful local information for residents and businesses.*
Buttons: View Latest Updates · Submit a Tip.

**3. Featured / breaking update panel.** A prominent card below the hero: category label, headline, short summary, time/date, optional image, "Read more". This is where urgent travel disruption, council news or breaking local information appears.

**4. Quick category tiles.** A row or grid of clickable cards — Latest Updates, News, Travel, Community, Events, Business. Stacks to 2-column or 1-column on mobile.

**5. Latest updates feed.** A vertical feed of article cards: title, category, short preview, date/time, optional thumbnail, read-more link. This is the core of the homepage and should always feel fresh.

**6. Trending / most read.** A smaller side section on desktop, or a block below the feed on mobile. Skip in v1 if analytics aren't in place.

**7. Community spotlight.** One featured card or two-to-three smaller cards — local event, business feature, volunteer story, school/community achievement, local win. Stops the site feeling like pure disruption news.

**8. Events preview.** A short list or cards with event title, date, location and link — community events, markets, family activities, borough events, school or charity events.

**9. Travel / alerts.** A dedicated block with two to four recent travel updates: roadworks, closures, bus issues, rail delays, weather disruptions. One of the most valuable local utilities.

**10. About preview.** *Dagenham Updates is an independent local information service covering Barking & Dagenham and surrounding areas. We share timely updates on news, travel, events, community notices, and public-interest stories.* CTA: Learn More.

**11. Submission / contact CTA.** *Have a local update, event, or tip to share? Send it to us.* Buttons: Submit a Tip · Contact Us.

**12. Social follow.** Instagram and X/Twitter icons and follow buttons. *Follow Dagenham Updates on social media for quick local updates.*

**13. Newsletter signup (optional for MVP).** Email field, subscribe button, short explanation: *Get local updates straight to your inbox.*

**14. Footer.** Home, Latest Updates, About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, social links. Copyright notice, brand statement and disclaimer: *Dagenham Updates is an independent local information service. Where possible, we verify content before publishing.*

**Desktop order:** header · hero · breaking/featured · category tiles · latest updates · community spotlight · events preview · travel/alerts · about preview · submit a tip · social follow · footer.

**Mobile order (tighter):** header · hero · breaking update · category tiles · latest updates · submit a tip · events or travel · about preview · footer.

**Style:** clean, news-like, easy to scan, minimal clutter, bold headlines, strong spacing, clear category labels, mobile-first. Avoid too many animations, heavy graphics, oversized text blocks, cluttered sidebars and random widgets.

**Core UX goal.** On landing, the user should immediately understand what Dagenham Updates is, what area it covers, what kind of updates it posts, where to find the latest news, and how to send a tip.
